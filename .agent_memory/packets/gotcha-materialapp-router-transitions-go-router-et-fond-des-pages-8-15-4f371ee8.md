# MaterialApp.router : transitions go_router et fond des pages (8.15)

> ShadApp.router was a bare WidgetsApp, so go router built every route as a NoTransitionPage: the app had no transition…

ShadApp.router was a bare WidgetsApp, so go_router built every route as a NoTransitionPage: the app had no transitions at all. Since 8.15, MaterialApp.router makes go_router build MaterialPage with the iOS slide on every route, including the ShellRoute. Rules now:
- The ShellRoute, the tab roots (/home, /colleges, /progress, /profile) and the entry screens (/splash, /welcome, /paywall) use pageBuilder: _instant (a NoTransitionPage) in app_router.dart, so switching tabs never slides.
- Screens pushed inside a tab keep the iOS transition and swipe-back.
- CgPage paints its own ground background, otherwise the screen underneath shows through during a slide.
- App.builder (collegaAppBuilder) sets a default DefaultTextStyle of CgType.body in ink; without it, a Text with no style inherits Material's alert style (red, yellow underline).
- Screen tests use WidgetsApp.router plus CgGoldenFrame, which hide these three problems. They are covered in test/core/router/app_router_test.dart and test/core/app_text_default_test.dart.
Evidence: Adversarial review 8.15: tab switch slid in like a pushed screen (CupertinoPageTransition on the shell page), and texts overlapped during push. Tests reproduced both, then passed after the fix.
Verified by: flutter test test/core (495), test/features/* all green
